# Waqf data requests (drafts - not sent)

## (a) Email / letter to the Karnataka State Board of Auqaf

**To:** The Chief Executive Officer, Karnataka State Board of Auqaf, Darul Auqaf, No. 6, Cunningham Road, Bengaluru - 560052
**Subject:** Request for list of registered masjids, dargahs and idgahs in Bengaluru Urban and Bengaluru Rural districts for a free community directory

Assalamu alaikum, respected Sir/Madam,

I am Kifayath Ulla, a resident of Bengaluru. I am building wheretopray (https://wheretopray.vercel.app), a free, non-commercial public directory that helps people find the nearest masjid, dargah, idgah or prayer room, with directions, qibla direction and prayer times. It has no advertising and no charges.

Public map sources list only about 560 places in Bengaluru, while the Board's records hold far more. To make the directory accurate, I request the following for Bengaluru Urban and Bengaluru Rural districts:

1. The list of registered waqf institutions of the type masjid, dargah, idgah and takia/musalla, with the Board registration number.
2. For each institution: name, full address, ward/locality, taluk and pincode.
3. GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) where they have been recorded, for example in the WAMSI or UMEED survey.
4. Whether the institution is currently functional, and the mutawalli or committee contact if the Board is willing to share it publicly.

A spreadsheet (Excel/CSV) is preferred, but a PDF or printed copy is also fine. I will credit the Karnataka State Board of Auqaf as the source on every listing, will not use the data commercially, and will correct any entry the Board asks me to. If the Board needs a formal application, an undertaking, or a fee, please let me know and I will provide it.

I would also be glad to share the finished directory with the Board so masjid committees can update their own timings.

JazakAllah khair for your time and help.

Yours sincerely,
Kifayath
